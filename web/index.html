How it works

Gemini never sees the raw match. Cheap deterministic code does the heavy lifting; the model only reasons about what needs vision and language.

What is measured, and what is estimated

Measured by code (deterministic)

  • Player positions on the court (4–8 point homography, camera-drift correction)
  • Court coverage, time near the net, movement speed
  • Joint angles from 33-point pose, e.g. knee flexion at the deepest planted lunge
  • When the ball is actually in play on this court

Estimated by Gemini (labelled * in the app)

  • Shot consistency, clutch factor, shot-style aggression, from a ~40 s reel
  • Injury-risk second opinion on the flagged frame
  • Commentary, caption and roast, written against the reel's real timeline

Honest limits

  • Demo footage is third-party broadcast video, not the PlayQ pod. Pod capture (₹5,000, 1080p/60 fps) is not demonstrated by it.
  • On overhead footage the far-court players are small and tracked less reliably; far-side positions are ±0.5–1 m.
  • Knee-risk bands and rating scales are heuristics, not medical advice. Valgus needs the player to face or turn away from the camera.
  • Player identity is the weakest link on multi-player broadcast video; per-player cards there are indicative.
